Oct 2026 Program Evaluation · Data Engineering · SQL

Appointment Attendance Evaluation

The reminder texts looked like a failure. They weren't.

A clinic's text reminders looked like they made people miss more appointments. I built a tested data pipeline over 110K appointments to find out why, then a model that flags who's most likely to miss so staff know who to call.

Read more ↓Show less ↑

The texts looked 11 points worse than sending nothing. The reason: the clinic only texts people who book at least 3 days out, and same-day bookers (who can never receive one) almost never miss. They were padding the comparison group. On the eligible group the result reverses to 5 points fewer missed appointments, not 11 more.

The pipeline rebuilds from the source CSV in one command across raw, staging, and feature layers, with 16 quality checks that halt the build instead of passing bad data downstream, plus a test suite that corrupts the database 10 different ways to prove each check actually fires. A logistic model then ranks who to call, surfacing no-shows at twice the base rate in the top 5%.

2026 Recommendation · RAG · Multimodal

Apateur

For the amateur behind the aperture.

Upload a photo you love and the camera gear you own. Apateur finds similar shots by real photographers, shows the settings they used, and tells you which of your lenses fits best.

Read more ↓Show less ↑

Visual search runs on CLIP embeddings over 22k photos stored in PostgreSQL with pgvector. For each match it returns the settings distributions real photographers actually used (f-stops, ISO, shutter speed), ranks your lenses for that shot type, and recommends what to buy next. Explanations come from a local Llama 3.2 model, grounded with RAG so it can't make up lens specs.

Mar – Oct 2026 Real-Time · Fraud Detection · MLOps

ShopSentry

Fraud detection that learns what normal looks like.

A real-time system that catches bots and fraud rings in simulated online-store traffic as it happens. It learns what normal shoppers look like from a small set of trusted sessions, then flags anything that doesn't fit.

Read more ↓Show less ↑

Events stream through Redpanda into Spark Structured Streaming, which turns them into 9 session features stored in Feast (Redis for live lookups, Parquet for training). Real fraud teams rarely know which sessions are fraud, so an autoencoder learns normal behavior from just 500 verified-normal sessions, and XGBoost learns from its labels for fast scoring.

Every approach is scored against held-out true labels, and an audit of my own pipeline caught duplicate session rows from Spark and a model that was quietly trained on the answers. Both are fixed. Served through FastAPI with an MLflow model registry, Prometheus metrics, and CI.

Jan – Dec 2025 Semantic Search · RAG · Capstone

Cooked

What can I make with these 4 ingredients?

A recipe app that answers "what can I make with what's in my fridge?" On a 4-person capstone team, I led the AI side: search over 3k+ real recipes and a chatbot that only recommends dishes that actually exist.

Read more ↓Show less ↑

Search uses transformer embeddings indexed in Pinecone, so it understands what you mean instead of matching exact keywords. The chatbot retrieves real recipes first and answers from them, with clickable results, and can adapt a recipe for dietary needs (gluten free, dairy free, vegetarian) without inventing a new one. LLM APIs (OpenAI, DeepSeek) filled in missing ingredient metadata across the full dataset.
